## 🚀 What It Is
Video Frame Extractor for Windows is a friendly desktop app that turns the clips already saved on your PC into still pictures. Whether you need one perfect frame, the closing shot of a clip, or a steady stream of frames spaced across a video, this frame extractor from video does the work in a few clicks and saves everything to a folder you choose.

> Load a clip, pick your extraction mode, and let Video Frame Extractor save the stills for you.

## ✨ Key Features
- 🎯 **Pick any single frame** — scrub to the exact moment in your video and export it as a still image.
- 🏁 **Grab the last frame** — capture the final frame of a clip instantly, perfect for closing shots.
- 🔁 **Every Nth frame** — set an interval and export a whole sequence of frames in one pass.
- 🖼️ **Native quality stills** — images come out at your video's own resolution, not a thumbnail.
- 📂 **Choose your output** — send saved frames to any folder in the image format you prefer.
